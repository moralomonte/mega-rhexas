Open beta

Help look for planets in public starlight.

Volunteers’ computers search public NASA TESS star data for planets passing in front of their stars. Yours can help. It downloads a few hundred stars, looks for a repeating dip, sends back a tiny result, and deletes the big files.

Sectors

TESS splits the sky into sectors. Each bar is that sector’s stars. A full bar is not a discovery. It means those stars have been checked.

What this is

A tiny dip

When a planet passes in front of its star, the star dims a tiny bit. That is a transit.

How a transit works

Two checks

Two different people search each batch. It counts when they see the same dip.

Why two people

Checked once

One person checks each batch for now. The two-person check will return as more people join.

Why one check

Unverified

A candidate stays unverified until astronomers confirm it. This site does not announce a discovery.
